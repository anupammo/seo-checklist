SEO Checklist Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

1. What SEO Checklist Reads

When you click the extension icon and run an analysis, SEO Checklist reads the currently active tab's page content — title, meta tags, headings, images, and hyperlinks — to generate your on-page SEO checklist and score. This happens entirely inside your browser.

SEO Checklist does not collect, store, or transmit any of the following to us or anyone else:

  • Personal user information (name, email, identifiers)
  • Authentication information (passwords, credentials)
  • Location, financial, or health data
  • Browsing history — only the single active tab is read, and only when you trigger an analysis
  • User activity such as clicks, scrolling, or keystrokes

Analysis results (score, checklist, links, image lists) exist only in the popup's memory while it's open and are discarded when the popup closes. Nothing about the pages you analyze is written to storage, and nothing is sent to any server we operate. The extension keeps a small amount of data on your device only (chrome.storage.local): your score history per page (URL, score, date and which checks failed, up to 10 checks per page and 200 pages) so it can show your progress, a count of analyses for the one-time rating request, and the version number for the one-time "What's new" message. This data never leaves your browser and is deleted when you remove the extension.

2. Outbound Requests (Broken Link & Image Checks)

To check for broken internal links and oversized images, SEO Checklist makes HEAD/GET requests from the page you are analyzing to the link and image URLs on that page's own site — the same URLs your browser would request if you clicked them yourself. This needs no extra permission.

Checking external links (and images hosted on other domains) is optional. Only if you click "Check external links and images" does Chrome ask you to grant access to other sites (optional host permissions http://*/*, https://*/*). You can remove that access at any time on the extension's details page in chrome://extensions.

These requests go directly from your browser to those third-party servers — they are never routed through, logged by, or visible to us.

3. External Testing Tools

When you click the "Test This Page In…" buttons (Structured Data, PageSpeed Insights, Facebook Debugger, or LinkedIn Post Inspector), you are redirected to Google, Facebook, or LinkedIn's own tools, with your current page URL passed along so the tool can pre-fill it. These are external services with their own privacy policies; we do not control or access any data they collect.

4. Permissions Explanation

SEO Checklist uses only the permissions required for its features:

  • activeTab: Access the currently active page only after you click the extension
  • scripting: Run analysis code on the active tab to evaluate on-page SEO elements
  • storage: Keep your score history and settings on your device only (see Section 1)
  • Optional host permissions (http://*/*, https://*/*): Only requested if you choose to check external links and images (see Section 2). Not granted at install.
