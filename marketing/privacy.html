SEO Checklist Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2026

1. Data Collection

SEO Checklist does not collect, store, or transmit:

  • Personal user information
  • Website content you analyze
  • Browsing history
  • Any other sensitive data

Note on External Tools: When you click the "Test Recent URL" buttons (Structured Data, PageSpeed Insights, or Facebook Debugger), you will be redirected to Google or Facebook's external services. These services have their own privacy policies. We do not control or access data collected by these third-party tools.

2. How We Process Information

All analysis occurs locally in your browser:

  • Page content is processed temporarily during analysis
  • No data is sent to our servers
  • Results are never stored persistently
  • Your current page URL is only used to open external testing tools in new tabs when you click those buttons

3. Permissions Explanation

The activeTab permission allows:

  • Reading the current webpage's DOM for SEO analysis
  • Functionality only when you click the extension icon

4. Changes to This Policy

Policy updates will be posted on this page with revised "Last Updated" dates.
