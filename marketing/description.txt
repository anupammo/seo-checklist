SEO Checklist - Free On-Page SEO Checker, Audit & PDF Report

Open any web page, click SEO Checklist, and get an instant on-page audit: a score out of 100, a ranked list of what to fix, and clear advice for every check. No sign-up, no account, and nothing you analyze leaves your browser.

WHAT YOU GET
✓ Score and Top fixes: see the issues with the biggest impact first
✓ Score history: re-check after a fix and see your progress (saved on your device only)
✓ Google search preview: desktop and mobile, with title and description width measured in pixels the way Google cuts them off. Edit the text live to test new versions.
✓ Social share preview: Open Graph and Twitter card check, with missing tags listed
✓ Headings outline: H1 to H6 tree with skipped levels highlighted
✓ Broken link checker for internal links, plus external links on request
✓ Images: file size, missing alt text and missing width/height
✓ Indexability: robots noindex/nofollow, canonical tag, page language
✓ Structured data types (JSON-LD and microdata) and Google Analytics / Tag Manager detection
✓ Printable PDF report and one-click copy of a text summary
✓ One-click links to Rich Results Test, PageSpeed Insights, Facebook Sharing Debugger, LinkedIn Post Inspector, Schema Validator, Wayback Machine, robots.txt and sitemap.xml
✓ Dark mode and keyboard shortcuts (Alt+Shift+S to open)

PRIVACY
The extension only reads the tab you analyze, when you open it. It does not ask for access to all websites at install. Checking external links is optional and asks for permission only when you choose it. No data is sent to us or anyone else.

WHO IT'S FOR
SEO specialists, content writers, web developers, marketers and agencies who want a fast, reliable check before publishing.
